ASSIGNMENT PLANNER - QUICK START\n\nFIRST TIME ONLY\n1. Extract this entire folder somewhere permanent, such as Documents\\Assignment Planner.\n2. Open the folder in File Explorer.\n3. Click the address bar, type cmd, and press Enter.\n4. Run: py -m pip install -r requirements.txt\n5. Double-click 'Launch Assignment Planner.bat'.\n\nEASY TASKBAR STARTUP\n1. Right-click 'Launch Assignment Planner.bat' and choose Show more options > Create shortcut.\n2. Move the shortcut to your Desktop.\n3. Right-click the shortcut and choose Show more options > Pin to taskbar.\n   If Pin to taskbar is not shown, pin the shortcut to Start or keep it on the Desktop.\n4. In future, click the pinned shortcut to start the planner. Keep the folder in the same location.\n\nIMPORT YOUR ASSIGNMENTS\n1. Open the app and expand 'Import assignments in bulk (JSON)'.\n2. Upload 'assignments_from_sheet.json'.\n3. Click 'Import assignments'.\n4. The app skips duplicates with the same assignment name and due date.\n\nIMPORTANT\n- Keep assignments.json in this folder; it stores your assignments and imported calendar.\n- Do not move only app.py or the launcher out of this folder.\n- The app uses your Google Calendar .ics export when imported.\n
